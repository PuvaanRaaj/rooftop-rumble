Connect gamepads before opening player setup; press a controller button if your browser has not detected it yet. Each keyboard layout and gamepad can join once. Tap Grab to pick up a fighter or prop, then tap again to throw. Shove also throws what you are holding. Tap jump or punch to escape a grab. Escape returns to the game menu.

Play together on the same computer. This version has no online multiplayer or touch controls. Use a current desktop browser with WebGL 2 support.
